# How we keep the portfolio moving

A simple routine, with people involved—not a fully autonomous system.

## One flow

1. **Find something worthwhile.** Dusk brings an idea; the Lab or Builder supplies useful research or an observed problem.
2. **Choose the next piece.** Keep one main project and a small skills queue. Use MVgrillz when planning is genuinely useful, not as a mandatory ceremony.
3. **Do the work in its existing home.** Skill Factory develops its skills; hermgainz keeps its separate project. MVskill-ops helps author or improve a skill.
4. **Try it and explain what happened.** Save a real example, what we contributed, what worked and what remains uncertain.
5. **Update the portfolio.** Add the useful result to the relevant project description and public material when appropriate.
6. **Create content from the work.** Maya receives the exact safe write-up and prepares a draft. Dusk reviews publication.
7. **Use feedback.** Improve, narrow, park or continue the piece based on what we learned.

## Who does what

| Who | Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Dusk | Direction, feedback and public decisions |
| AI Research Engineer Lab | Specific research questions and findings |
| Builder | Develop, try and document the relevant work |
| Maya | Content drafts based on documented work |

## Light upkeep

Once a week, check what moved, what is worth sharing and the next useful action. Request content when there is something meaningful to say. Scheduled automation is optional, not a condition for this routine to work.
